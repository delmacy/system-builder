# Business and Sustainability Model

System Builder is designed so commercial retention results from value, not artificial technical captivity.

## Open platform

The platform may be self-hosted and used independently according to its eventual license. Openness must also exist architecturally through portable data, documented contracts, replaceable integrations and autonomous runtimes.

## Revenue layers

Potential paid services include:

- process elicitation and mirroring;
- business/process engineering;
- implementation and customization;
- data migration;
- legacy integration;
- hosting and managed operations;
- backup and recovery;
- observability;
- security and upgrades;
- help desk and SLA;
- maintenance;
- process/system evolution;
- training and consulting;
- premium service contracts.

## Service philosophy

A client must be technically able to operate without the original service provider. The commercial objective is that the client prefers continued management because of trust, domain knowledge, responsiveness and quality.

## Three service modes

1. **Autonomous:** client self-hosts and maintains.
2. **Assisted:** client operates, provider supports/evolves.
3. **Managed:** provider handles infrastructure, updates, backup, observability and support.

## Dogfooding

The organization behind System Builder should use its own factory to create and operate its own systems. Real internal/client systems are the primary source of engineering pressure and reusable improvements.

Generic knowledge may return to the catalogs; client-sensitive data and proprietary business information do not.
