# WBS — 49 Disaster Recovery / Continuity

## 49.0 Disaster Recovery / Continuity Plane
### 49.1 Continuity design
- **49.1.1** Classificar critical services/data e definir RTO/RPO.
- **49.1.2** Mapear dependencies/failure modes e degraded operation.
- **49.1.3** Definir backup/failover/recovery architecture por profile.
### 49.2 Recovery operations
- **49.2.1** Automatizar/verificar backups e integrity checks.
- **49.2.2** Implementar restore/failover/rollback procedures.
- **49.2.3** Preservar secrets/config/infra requirements para recovery.
### 49.3 Drills/readiness
- **49.3.1** Executar restore/DR drills em cadence definida.
- **49.3.2** Medir achieved RTO/RPO e data loss.
- **49.3.3** Registrar gaps/corrective actions e evidence.
