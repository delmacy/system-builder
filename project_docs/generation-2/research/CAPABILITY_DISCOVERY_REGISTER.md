## Mandatory research hypotheses
`G2-RESEARCH-HYPOTHESIS-EXECUTABLE-CAPABILITY-COMPOSITION-AND-CUMULATIVE-CONTEXT` and `G2-RESEARCH-HYPOTHESIS-TRANSACTION-CONSISTENCY-CONCURRENCY` remain **USER-DIRECTED / MANDATORY_RESEARCH / NOT_A_PROMOTED_CAPABILITY** in their concept dossiers.

`G2-RESEARCH-HYPOTHESIS-TOPOLOGY-BUILD-RUNTIME-REALIZATION` — **USER-DIRECTED / MANDATORY_RESEARCH / NOT_A_PROMOTED_CAPABILITY**. See `project_docs/generation-2/research/concepts/TOPOLOGY_BUILD_RUNTIME_REALIZATION.md`. Research must separate Semantic Architecture, Runtime Topology, Build Topology, Deployment Topology and Observed Topology; test `DeploymentUnit`/`BuildTarget`/placement/isolation/scaling/resource profiles without equating them to containers; and prove that semantically distinct modules may share a simple runtime while heavy workloads can split and replicate independently. The user-directed product constraint is mature-system semantics with simple-system ergonomics: a common low-load business system must not inherit distributed-systems/Kubernetes ceremony merely because the canonical model can also represent mature HA/scaled deployments. Providerization must be justified by reuse of proven build/orchestration/deployment mechanics: SB owns portable topology requirements/evidence and should compose ready-made providers instead of rebuilding generic orchestration machinery. Build, Artifact/Release, Deployment/Runtime, Provider/Binding, Security/Recovery, Observability, Secrets/Configuration, Standards/API, Lifecycle, Transaction/Consistency, Executable Composition and AGWS/UI must cross-test this hypothesis. Negative-Space Review and Capability Synthesis must explicitly dispose it by evidence.

`G2-RESEARCH-HYPOTHESIS-TENANT-FLEET-EDGE-INGRESS-ROUTING` — **USER-DIRECTED / MANDATORY_RESEARCH / NOT_A_PROMOTED_CAPABILITY**. See `project_docs/generation-2/research/concepts/TENANT_FLEET_EDGE_INGRESS_ROUTING.md`. Research must distinguish Tenant/System/Environment identity from DomainBinding, ExternalExposure, IngressRoute, TenantTopologyBinding, RuntimePool, TLS and backend realization; support shared runtime pools, shared servers with dedicated runtimes and distributed/dedicated customer topologies; treat compute/data/network/credential/storage/cache/event/observability/failure isolation as separate profile dimensions; and prove deterministic routing and cross-tenant isolation for fleets of 20+ customers. Reverse proxies/gateways such as Caddy, Traefik, NGINX, Gateway API, Envoy, Cloudflare and ALB are provider realizations, not canonical tenant identity. Tenant migration must preserve hostname/semantic identity while changing backend placement through governed cutover/recovery evidence.

## Authoritative architecture proof / quality method
`project_docs/generation-2/research/ARCHITECTURE_PROOF_QUALITY_METHOD.md` is mandatory and applies retroactively to all active capabilities and hypotheses. Every material architectural claim must eventually have explicit positive, negative/adversarial and evidence-producing proof obligations. Existing dossiers that predate the method acquire proof-backfill debt; Capability Synthesis and `PLANNING_E_PRODUCT_PROOF_ACCEPTANCE` must reconcile and convert those obligations into executable test definitions. A claim such as provider replaceability, tenant isolation, workflow durability, deterministic build, non-amplifying authority or runtime autonomy is not considered fully accepted by documentation alone.
