`G2-CAPABILITY-CANDIDATE-ADAPTIVE-GOVERNED-WORK-SURFACES` — **CORE / PROMOTED_TO_ACTIVE_RESEARCH_TAXONOMY**. Dedicated evidence establishes a material capability distinct from generic low-code UI.
