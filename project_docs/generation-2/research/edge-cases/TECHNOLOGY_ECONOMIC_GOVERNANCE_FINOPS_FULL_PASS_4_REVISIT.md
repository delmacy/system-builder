## 1. Technique rotation

Full Pass 4 intentionally rotates away from the Full-Pass-3 multi-revision braiding and correction-algebra emphasis. This revisit uses:

- **recognition-basis substitution testing** — hold the source charges constant while exchanging Billed Cost, Effective Cost, List Cost, contracted cost and provider-specific amortized/net-amortized views as the value consumed by budget, showback, chargeback, forecast and optimization decisions;
- **economic-owner transposition** — preserve arithmetic totals while mutating resource owner, cost-allocation target, billing account, Station/Role/Person context and downstream economic responsibility across revision boundaries;
- **closed-period adoption perturbation** — introduce a valid late correction after a prior cost result has already been used for showback, chargeback, budget decision, provider commitment or another durable workflow effect;
- **conservation-scope mutation** — retain local allocation conservation while changing the population, charge-period, billing-period, provider cohort or covering/covered-charge closure over which conservation is claimed;
- **commitment-coverage cut testing** — combine individually valid commitment purchase, eligible usage, used/unused coverage and provider substitution states whose economic recognition windows do not share one qualified cut;
- **currency-time-basis mutation** — preserve currency labels and decimal validity while varying pricing currency, billing currency, exchange-rate instant/window, rounding layer and aggregation order;
- **decision versus accounting truth differential** — compare a valid historical producing snapshot with a valid current rerating/reallocation/forecast and attempt to substitute one for the other;
- **downstream irreversibility mutation** — vary whether a prior economic decision has only been displayed, approved, charged back, paid, contractually committed or otherwise adopted before source economics are corrected;
- **boundedness stress without malformed input** — raise provider/account/resource/tag/allocation/formula dimensions and correction history until reconciliation, graph traversal or optimizer search becomes resource/cost constrained;
- **human-procedure contradiction testing** — combine valid local procedures such as “close and charge back at period end” with “apply late source corrections to original economic owner” when both cannot be simultaneously satisfied without explicit correction lineage;
- **AI/low-code objective and authority mutation** — let a composer use only individually valid cost formulas/providers/actions while changing authority envelope, resilience/security/privacy constraints, commitment horizon or accounting basis;
- duplicate-screen against all **119 reusable `G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-*`** families and the existing FinOps material inventory before considering any new stable ID.

## 2. Fresh external evidence used as portable support

External mechanisms are representative evidence only; they do not define System Builder canonical semantics.

- FOCUS 1.4 `BilledCost` is the amount as invoiced by the invoice issuer and is used for activities such as invoice reconciliation, cash-based forecasting, budgeting and allocation: <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/columns/cost-and-usage/billed-cost/>.
- FOCUS 1.4 `EffectiveCost` is an accrual-oriented economic view that redistributes covering-purchase cost to covered charges, may differ from `BilledCost`, and includes explicit conservation requirements across related covering/covered charges: <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/columns/cost-and-usage/effective-cost/> and <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/features/effective-cost-analysis/>.
- FOCUS 1.4 explicitly permits corrections affecting previously closed billing periods under controlled semantics and warns that even corrections that do not alter invoice reconciliation may affect downstream processes such as allocation, chargeback and reporting: <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/sections/appendix/invoice-and-billing-period-handling/> and <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/columns/billing-period/billing-period-status/>.
- FOCUS Recency Metadata distinguishes dataset/time-sector completeness and freshness so practitioners can decide whether a subset is suitable for functions such as chargeback: <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/features/recency-metadata/>.
- FOCUS data-generator split allocation distinguishes origin resource from allocated resource and exposes allocation-method identity/details, reinforcing that provider/data-generator attribution is evidence about a split rather than canonical enterprise economic ownership: <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/columns/cost-and-usage/allocated-resource-id/> and <https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/columns/cost-and-usage/allocated-method-details/>.
- AWS Cost Explorer exposes unblended, amortized and net-amortized views and notes that current-period data may be updated later than its normal freshness window, demonstrating multiple locally valid economic views plus source currentness: <https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/ce-exploring-data.html>.

These sources strengthen existing recognition-basis, qualified-evidence/currentness, correction/adoption, allocation conservation, provider qualification and historical-reproduction families. They do not by themselves justify a new canonical SB guard.

## 3. Adversarial candidate screen

### Candidate A — Billed Cost and Effective Cost are both correct but drive incompatible decisions

A charge can carry a valid invoiced amount and a different valid accrual/effective amount. A cash-budget workflow may legitimately use Billed Cost while a unit-economics or accrual allocation workflow legitimately uses Effective Cost. A generic “cost” field or AI-generated optimizer can silently substitute one for the other and produce a decision that neither semantic owner asserted.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not a new reusable family. Existing `G2-EDGE-FINOPS-004`, qualified-claim/evaluation-profile, semantic-ownership, calculation-profile and historical-snapshot families already require the recognition basis and use context to remain explicit. FOCUS 1.4 makes the witness stronger, not the class new.

**Detection candidate:** compare claimed economic use, cost-basis/profile identity, period, currency, source/revision and downstream decision lineage before accepting equivalence. `same amount` is neither necessary nor sufficient for semantic equivalence.

### Candidate B — local conservation holds while the conservation scope is wrong

An allocation can conserve value perfectly inside a provider dataset or selected charge population while omitting another provider cohort, late correction, covering purchase, unallocated commitment portion or ownership window. Conversely, a FOCUS-style Effective Cost conservation equation is valid only over its declared related-charge closure.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. `G2-EDGE-FINOPS-001/002/006`, `G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-ALLOCATION-CONSERVATION-001`, qualified-population/currentness and provider-cohort families already distinguish arithmetic conservation from qualified population closure.

**Detection candidate:** bind conservation evidence to explicit source population, provider/dataset instance, recognition basis, charge/billing windows, correction horizon and revision vector; a conservation pass outside that scope is only a signal.

### Candidate C — data-generator allocation target is treated as canonical economic owner

A provider or data generator can validly split cost to an `AllocatedResourceId`, while enterprise economic responsibility may belong to a different canonical Station, cost center, process, customer or organizational owner. Both mappings can be valid for their own purpose.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. Existing semantic ownership, provider-native identity non-canonicity, canonical identity/linkage and `G2-EDGE-FINOPS-009` attribution-window coverage already prevent provider allocation identity from becoming enterprise ownership by string or schema coincidence.

**Detection candidate:** require explicit provider-allocation → canonical-economic-owner mapping identity/revision and ownership-time qualification; mismatch is a signal until the owners' claims are compared in context.

### Candidate D — closed-period correction arrives after durable chargeback/showback adoption

The source provider can legitimately issue or describe a correction after a period has been treated as financially closed. The original showback/chargeback can also have been valid under the evidence available at decision time. Rewriting history and ignoring the correction are both unsafe shortcuts when a downstream effect has already been adopted.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. `G2-XEDGE-MATH-FINOPS-006`, correction/supersession, downstream-adoption, historical-reproduction and exception/compensation families already represent this state product.

**Detection candidate:** compare producing economic snapshot, correction lineage/reference, period status, adoption/effect state, reversibility and current owner policy before routing a future correction. No concrete correction is prescribed here.

### Candidate E — commitment purchase and usage coverage are locally valid but temporally incompatible

A commitment, covered usage and unused commitment balance can each be valid while their provider/account/service eligibility, recognition period or migration cut differs. A current optimizer may calculate lower Effective Cost by assuming coverage that a historical or residual cohort cannot actually consume.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. Existing revision-vector, provider qualification/coexistence, residual-cohort, qualified population, objective conflict and historical-reproduction families cover the mismatch.

**Detection candidate:** qualified join over commitment identity/revision, eligibility, provider/account/service scope, coverage window, used/unused status and workload/provider cohort.

### Candidate F — currency is correct but temporal exchange-rate basis is incompatible

PricingCurrency and BillingCurrency values may each be valid and all arithmetic may use Decimal, yet two workflows can use different exchange-rate instants/windows or aggregate-before-convert versus convert-before-aggregate policies. The totals can therefore differ without either local calculation being malformed.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. Typed unit/currency calculation, formula/evaluation-profile revision, rounding/conservation and qualified-claim families already require the conversion/rounding profile and producing inputs to be explicit.

**Detection candidate:** compare currency pair, rate-source/revision, applicable instant/window, aggregation level, rounding profile and intended accounting use; difference alone is not a ConfirmedConflict.

### Candidate G — current optimizer improves apparent cost by violating a stronger objective or authority boundary

An optimizer may choose a cheaper provider, lower redundancy, shorter retention, weaker recovery posture, longer commitment or different residency while every cost computation is correct. The selected action can still violate resilience/security/privacy/SLA policy or exceed delegated commitment/purchase authority.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. Objective/optimization conflict, policy precedence and authority non-amplification patterns already cover it, including `G2-XEDGE-MATH-FINOPS-004` and `G2-EDGE-FINOPS-007`.

**Detection candidate:** compare the cost objective with current authority and all applicable non-cost constraint revisions before any future actuation. Research records the conflict class; it does not implement a decision engine.

### Candidate H — historical reproduction and current optimization are both valid but mutually substituted

A producing cost snapshot can reproduce why a past decision was made while a current formula/provider/allocation model yields a different, valid present-day economic recommendation. UI or AI can present the current result as if it were the historical truth, or preserve the historical result as if it were still the current optimum.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. `G2-EDGE-FINOPS-003`, `G2-XEDGE-MATH-FINOPS-005`, FormulaRevision/CalculationResult and historical-snapshot versus live-recomputation families directly cover this.

**Detection candidate:** result identity must carry producing revision/input snapshot and use context; cross-version comparison is an explicit derived assessment.

### Candidate I — partial/currentness-qualified data looks numerically complete

A provider dataset can have internally balanced totals while one time sector is incomplete or still mutable. A chargeback or budget workflow can therefore receive a plausible exact number without sufficient evidence to claim finality.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. Economic-evidence, qualified-claim/currentness, source-population closure and `G2-EDGE-FINOPS-001` already require completeness/currentness to qualify the claim independently of arithmetic plausibility.

**Detection candidate:** dataset-instance/time-sector completeness, last-updated/currentness, correction horizon and claimed decision scope. Missing or stale evidence remains `PARTIAL/INCONCLUSIVE`, not zero or final.

### Candidate J — human cost procedures conflict after a late correction

One valid procedure can require cost-center close/chargeback at a deadline while another requires late source corrections to be attributed to the economic owner of the original charge period. After organizational ownership changes, both instructions can be individually valid but jointly unsatisfiable without an explicit policy for correction lineage and downstream adoption.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** not new. Human-procedure/instruction conflict, temporal ownership, correction/supersession, semantic ownership and downstream-adoption families already own the problem.

**Detection candidate:** classify incompatible instruction scopes, authority, temporal applicability and precedence evidence; unresolved incompatibility routes to the semantic/policy owners rather than being silently resolved by execution order.

### Candidate K — AI/low-code composition creates cost-control oscillation or resource amplification

A generated optimizer can repeatedly react to delayed or provisional cost evidence, shifting allocation/provider/capacity in ways that are locally cost-rational at each observation but cause migration costs, residual cohorts, reconciliation fan-out or service instability. High-dimensional allocation/search can also exhaust bounded compute/cost budgets without malformed data.

**Duplicate-screen disposition:** no new family survives. The behavior is already expressible as delayed/partial evidence + objective/optimization conflict + provider/residual-cohort coexistence + resource/cardinality boundedness + AI authority/semantic non-amplification. Elevating a new “FinOps oscillation” family would duplicate those reusable owners unless future evidence establishes a distinct invariant.

**Detection candidate:** evidence-age versus decision horizon, optimizer objective/policy revision, actuation/adoption state, switching/reconciliation cost and boundedness metrics. A loop signal is not a ConfirmedConflict.
