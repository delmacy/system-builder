## 2. Evidence refresh

Fresh evidence continues to support qualified identity/topology rather than one universal truth object.

- Kubernetes now documents Pod `metadata.generation` separately from `status.observedGeneration`; different status fields may correspond to current or previous sync-loop generations. This is direct industrial evidence that declared revision, observed revision and effective runtime state cannot be collapsed even inside one object model.
- Crossplane documents `cannot determine creation result` when an external resource may have been created but the provider could not durably record its external identity. It deliberately halts reconciliation to avoid duplicate external resources. This reinforces `attempt/acceptance != known external identity/effect != convergence`, and shows why ambiguity must survive a generic envelope.
- OpenTelemetry defines `service.name` as a logical service identity, `service.instance.id` as a concrete instance identity, `service.version` as artifact/version metadata, and deployment environment as a separate dimension. A telemetry vocabulary therefore demonstrates rather than eliminates topology/identity dimensions.

Portable conclusion: a useful UCA can standardize typed references, qualification envelopes and relations, but must not infer equivalence across semantic, build, deployment, runtime, provider, site or evidence topologies merely because names or IDs coincide.

Activation: two references serialize to the same convenient capability key/name while one differs in semantic owner/profile/revision or client/site applicability. A consumer caches or joins by the projected key.

Disposition: material signal, but duplicate of canonical-vs-realization identity collapse, qualified-claim loss and revision-vector truncation. No new ConflictPattern.

### B — Semantic topology and deployment/runtime topology evolve at different rates

Activation: graph revision N+1 changes capability dependencies while deployment/runtime cohorts remain on N, and Fleet/Graph Explorer renders only the latest semantic topology.

Incompatible claims: `dependency removed/added in current model` versus `old in-flight/runtime/provider cohorts still produce/consume effects under prior topology`.

Detection candidates: revision-pinned dependency closure, cohort inventory, build/deployment/runtime lineage, currentness-qualified projection.

Disposition: duplicate of temporal/revision coexistence, residual cohort, evidence currentness and topology/projection families. No new ConflictPattern.

### C — healthy local nodes conceal an unstable queue-network cut

Activation: each capability reports acceptable utilization while a shared downstream provider/queue/service-rate bottleneck is omitted from the aggregate operational view; retries/fan-out amplify arrival rate beyond sustainable service rate.

Incompatible claims: `components healthy/current utilization acceptable` versus `network stability margin negative or unknown`.

Detection candidates: queue-network cut/bottleneck analysis, arrival/service rate qualification, backlog trend, retry/fan-out contribution, provider quota/currentness.

Disposition: duplicate of resource/capacity, queue/backpressure, multidimensional-vector and false-convergence families. `current utilization != sustainable capacity != stability margin`. No new ConflictPattern.

### D — Elicitation says “resolved” while semantic owner or production-readiness evidence is absent

Activation: a master wizard/AI obtains textual answers sufficient to generate a capability model, but stakeholder coverage, source-of-truth, authority, failure/recovery, SLO, reconciliation, evidence/currentness or acceptance proof remains unresolved.

Incompatible claims: `question answered / feature model generated` versus `semantic understanding sufficient for architecture/implementation/publish-operation`.

Detection candidates: multidimensional coverage state, critical unanswered questions, contradiction disposition, evidence/currentness, Production Readiness Coverage.

Disposition: duplicate of evidence sufficiency/currentness, semantic ownership, false completeness and AI non-amplification. Reinforces separate gates `sufficient for abstraction`, `candidate architecture`, `implementation`, and `publish/operation`. No new ConflictPattern.

### E — Fleet projection joins heterogeneous revisions into one apparently authoritative topology

Activation: exported telemetry from multiple builds/deployments/sites is aggregated by logical capability identity without preserving source build/deployment/runtime/provider revision and evidence horizon.

Incompatible claims: `Fleet says capability X has topology/state Y` versus multiple local authoritative realities at different revisions/currentness.

Detection candidates: build/deployment-aware telemetry lineage, local evidence anchoring, source revision/currentness, client/site context.

Disposition: duplicate of evidence/projection currentness, revision-vector truncation and authority non-amplification. Fleet remains non-authoritative by default. No new ConflictPattern.

### F — Integration-plane provider feature label strengthens into physical actuation authority

Activation: provider exposes read/provision/sync and actuation under one SDK/profile/credential; generic UCA operation or AI composition maps a broad provider method into an apparently equivalent capability use.

Incompatible claims: `integration/governance operation supported` versus `authorized to cause physical/media effect`.

Detection candidates: operation-class semantics, provider capability matrix, explicit site/resource/authority scope, unsupported-scope diagnostics and specialized-system owner evidence.

Disposition: duplicate of provider semantic mismatch, confused deputy and authority non-amplification. Physical actuation remains non-goal/default-denied pending a separate later Planning C decision. No new ConflictPattern.

- What is the canonical semantic owner and exact identity tuple?

- Which topology is being described, and which other topologies can legitimately differ?
- What evidence establishes currentness and source-of-truth, and what may remain UNKNOWN?

- Which SLO/failure/recovery/reconciliation/rollback obligations exist and who owns them?

- Which artifact/test/product proof would justify the requested sufficiency gate?

A textual answer alone does not move these dimensions to `RESOLVED` without applicable evidence/currentness and contradiction disposition.
