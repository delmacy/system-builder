## External evidence checked

- AWS S3 Object Lock continues to distinguish retention periods and legal holds; both can coexist, and protection is attached to a specific object version. A legal hold remains until explicitly removed. Lifecycle processing can add delete markers while a locked version remains undeletable. Replication can copy locked objects and lock metadata asynchronously. These semantics reinforce obligation-set, version-scope and population-convergence distinctions rather than establishing a new portable conflict class.

This directly reinforces currentness and recovery-versus-residency requalification already represented by `G2-EDGE-PRIVACY-004`, `006`, `G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-RESIDENCY-PLACEMENT-001` and `ERASURE-RECOVERY-001`.
