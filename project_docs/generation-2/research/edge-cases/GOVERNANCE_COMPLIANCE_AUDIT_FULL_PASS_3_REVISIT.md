## Full-Pass-3 technique rotation

The pass deliberately used techniques different from the first two passes:

- **presence-semantics obligation metamorphism**: substitute `ABSENT`, `UNSET`, explicit `null`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, inherited/defaulted and redacted values at obligation/evidence boundaries and compare effective control meaning;
- **control-revision product braids**: compose control revision, policy revision, waiver revision, assessment method revision and evidence revision into mixed cuts rather than testing each dimension independently;
- **evidence shadowing and provenance fracture**: let newer but narrower evidence obscure older broader evidence, and let provider attestations coexist with canonical observations without granting either accidental ownership;
- **waiver lease permutations**: grant/expire/revoke/renew a bounded exception while work, assessment, remediation and review remain in flight;
- **closure-state lattice mutation**: permute `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `ATTEMPTED`, `EFFECTIVE`, `VALIDATED`, `SUPERSEDED` and `UNKNOWN` rather than treating closure as Boolean;
- **Enterprise/Station/Role/Person SoD folding**: compose inherited and local control scopes so individually valid assignments can still create aggregate self-approval or orphan responsibility;
- **distributed enforcement cuts**: inject `PARTIAL/UNKNOWN`, offline cohorts and residual provider cohorts after policy/control change;
- **objective inversion**: combine compliance, availability, cost and deadline objectives and test whether local optimization silently selects precedence;
- **cardinality/resource subtraction**: increase evidence/control/object populations while reducing assessment capacity and currentness horizon;
- **AI/low-code authority-delta mutation**: compose safe control/evidence/waiver operations into generated flows and compare effective authority, scope and evidentiary claims before versus after composition.

## External evidence calibration

Fresh standards evidence reinforces existing catalogue semantics rather than introducing a new class. NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5 explicitly treats control assessment as determining whether controls are implemented, meet objectives and achieve desired outcomes, and its continuous-monitoring assessment objectives include accuracy, currency and availability of monitoring results. NIST SP 800-137 and the RMF Monitor step treat continuous monitoring as ongoing situational awareness and repeated assessment of control effectiveness, while NIST IR 8011 Rev. 1 distinguishes desired state, actual state, test boundaries, data quality and testable controls. These sources support the existing separation between an obligation/control declaration, evidence about it, currentness of that evidence, and a qualified compliance conclusion.
