## Techniques materially different from Full Pass 1

This revisit emphasized composition mutation rather than replaying the seven original scenarios:

1. **Revision-vector omission mutation** — remove one independently changing dimension at a time from schema/workflow/runtime/provider/contract/policy/formula/credential/client compatibility evidence, then test whether a scalar readiness claim survives incorrectly.
2. **Temporal evidence braid** — interleave readiness/validation evidence with later writes, policy/trust/authority revisions and offline-cohort reappearance to test timeless-currentness assumptions.
3. **Cutover race braid** — interleave new-provider adoption, old-provider delayed callbacks, residual credentials, old clients, retries and control-plane cutover to test false drainage.
4. **Withdrawal/in-flight differential** — distinguish prohibition of new use from support required by legitimate in-flight work, callbacks, human procedures and scheduled/offline execution.
5. **Rollback dependency subtraction** — retain the historical release while independently changing data/schema/config/provider/trust/privacy/credential/runtime or external effects, then challenge eligibility.
6. **Supersession lineage mutation** — apply valid correction/backfill/supersession while selectively removing producing revision, formula, policy or authority lineage to test false historical continuity.
7. **Ambiguous migration-effect braid** — interleave timeout, partial checkpoint, provider substitution and retry under changing idempotency scope/horizon.
8. **Resource/cardinality stress** — grow cohort/revision/dependency graphs and partial-progress sets to test whether exhaustion can be mistaken for complete migration.
9. **AI/low-code evolution differential** — generate syntactically coherent migration plans while varying current authority, owner semantics, rollback qualification, evidence completeness and residual-cohort assumptions.
