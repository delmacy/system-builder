### 2.2 Audit/provenance completeness versus authority or causal proof

Probed exact lineage, audit events, policy decision logs and evidence bundles where recorded provenance is complete enough to reconstruct *what was observed* but not enough to prove authority, necessity or causation.

External evidence supports this distinction. OPA decision logs can carry decision IDs, bundle revisions, inputs, timestamps and results, which strengthens traceability but does not itself prove that the decision was semantically correct or that downstream business effects occurred. OpenLineage now provides explicit lineage facets so consumers need not infer a Cartesian product of all inputs and outputs, reinforcing `lineage relation != causal proof`.

Disposition: duplicate-screen into `G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-PROOF-CLAIM-CONFLATION-001`, `G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-PROVENANCE-EDGE-OVERATTRIBUTION-001` and existing evidence/currentness families. No new ConflictPattern.
