## 1. Full-Pass-3 method

This revisit deliberately used techniques materially different from Full Passes 1 and 2:

1. **evidence-state projection mutation** — replace a rich owner result (`APPLIED / NOT_APPLIED / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN / INCONCLUSIVE`) with progressively simpler work-surface presentations and test whether the surface accidentally strengthens the claim;
2. **instruction-set intersection analysis** — combine independently valid Enterprise, Station, Role, process, human-procedure and AI instructions and test whether the effective set is contradictory, impossible or changes authority by ordering;
3. **scope-partition perturbation** — vary Person/Role/Station/tenant subject and target scope independently while preserving individually valid identity and authorization decisions;
4. **long-lived surface cut analysis** — hold a surface open across policy, workflow, schema, provider, identity and Station revisions and test whether renderability or prior validation becomes actuation authority;
5. **offline/resume and supersession braid** — suspend a surface, change canonical state/authority elsewhere, then resume old intent and compare replay eligibility with current semantic ownership;
6. **human/AI plan-delta analysis** — compare individually authorized AI suggestions and human-approved steps against the aggregate plan, including shared/personal automation promotion;
7. **negative-space duplicate screen** against all 115 reusable ConflictPatterns before admitting any new local or cluster finding.

## 2. Evidence refresh

Portable evidence was used as adversarial support, not as target-architecture prescription:

- W3C WAI-ARIA guidance distinguishes presentation/interaction semantics from actual operability. `aria-disabled` can remain focusable, and hidden/presentational semantics can diverge from what users discover through different interaction paths. This reinforces that visible/disabled/hidden surface state is neither authorization nor canonical domain truth.
- OpenFGA documents immutable authorization models and recommends pinning a specific authorization-model ID; migration guidance also shows that tuples can become invalid under a newer model and be ignored. This reinforces that a long-lived surface cannot infer authority currentness from a once-valid model/context.
- OWASP AI-agent guidance recommends least privilege, per-action authorization, context isolation, human review for high-impact actions and bounded tool chains. This reinforces that an AI recommendation or multi-step plan cannot acquire authority merely because each step is individually available.

## 3. Duplicate-screen result

### 3.1 Evidence-state projection

Candidate: a surface receives `PARTIAL`, `UNKNOWN` or `INCONCLUSIVE` evidence but renders a binary green/success state, thereby causing an operator or AI to continue as if the underlying claim were confirmed.

Disposition: **already materially covered** by projection-semantics, qualified-claim/currentness, presentation-authority and ambiguous-effect families, plus `G2-EDGE-UI-010` and AGWS provider-effect handling. No new edge class survives.

### 3.2 Contradictory human/process/AI instructions

Candidate: Enterprise instruction A, Station procedure B and AI recommendation C are each valid in isolation but jointly prescribe incompatible actions or ordering.

Disposition: **already materially covered** by `HUMAN-INSTRUCTION`, policy/precedence, work-priority projection, objective conflict and AI/low-code composition families. The activation conditions, owners, detection route and false-positive risk are already catalogued. No new reusable pattern is justified.

### 3.3 Cross-Station / cross-tenant scope partition

Candidate: subject identity is valid, role membership is valid, target resource is valid and an action is valid, but the assembled surface binds pieces from different Station/tenant contexts and presents an authority path that never existed as one qualified scope.

Disposition: **already materially covered** by multitenant-scope, effective-identity, permission-composition, qualified-claim/currentness and AGWS stale-context families. This is a strong cluster test but not a new class.

### 3.4 Long-lived surface across semantic revisions

Candidate: a surface remains open while policy/workflow/schema/provider or Station state changes, and prior renderability/validation is reused at actuation.

Disposition: **already covered** by AGWS stale-authority/currentness (`G2-EDGE-AGWS-001`), historical surface reactivation (`G2-EDGE-AGWS-009`), UI aggregate-cut (`G2-EDGE-UI-008`) and revision-vector/currentness families.

### 3.5 Offline/resumed intent

Candidate: an offline/resumed work surface replays a locally valid action after cancellation, supersession, authority loss or a later canonical effect.

Disposition: **already covered** by `G2-EDGE-UI-009`, residual-cohort, replay-eligibility, correction/supersession, ambiguous-effect/idempotency and currentness families.

### 3.6 Shared versus personal automation promotion

Candidate: a personal automation is valid for one actor and its individual steps are valid, but promotion to Role/Station/shared scope changes authority, recipient set, provider use or workflow semantics.

Disposition: **already covered** by `G2-EDGE-AGWS-004`, permission/automation composition, provider-composition authority and target-scope currentness families.

### 3.7 Individually valid human/AI steps compose into unsafe outcome

Candidate: identity, authorization, Station membership, AGWS exposure and AI tool availability all validate independently, yet their aggregate sequence achieves a transition or scope expansion not authorized as a whole.

Disposition: **already covered** by permission-composition, automation-composition, low-code materialization, AI non-amplification and the existing `Identity × Authorization × Station × AGWS × AI` scenarios. No genuinely new interaction family remains after N-wise comparison.

### 3.8 Resource/cardinality pressure

Candidate: a valid generated/adaptive surface expands cards, actions, filters, evidence or AI options until evaluation truncates and required semantics disappear.

Disposition: **already covered** by `G2-EDGE-AGWS-006`, resource-boundedness and UI projection semantics. No new class.
