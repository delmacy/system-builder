## Full-Pass-4 technique rotation

Techniques were deliberately changed from Full Passes 1-3:

- **counterfactual evidence recomposition**: hold each evidence item locally valid while changing which subset is visible to the assessor, then test whether the aggregate conclusion strengthens without justification;
- **obligation dependency graph inversion**: reverse dependency/precedence edges among individually valid controls and compensating controls to expose circular applicability or impossible closure conditions;
- **negative-evidence asymmetry**: compare `no adverse evidence observed` with `evidence that the adverse condition is absent`, including offline and sampled populations;
- **temporal SoD collapse**: let request, approval, exception, review and closure roles change across time so no single step violates policy while aggregate history can still become self-review/self-approval;
- **assessment cut non-commutativity**: permute policy revision, control revision, exception revision, evidence cut, remediation cut and reviewer authority cut and compare conclusions;
- **distributed closure counterfactuals**: keep remediation ACK valid while selectively withholding postcondition evidence from residual/offline cohorts;
- **provider-claim semantic subtraction**: progressively remove provider report scope, period, responsibility boundary and canonical subject mapping while retaining a syntactically valid provider compliance claim;
- **human-procedure braid**: compose audit, remediation, exception and operations instructions that are individually valid but may imply incompatible execution/review ordering;
- **objective-frontier perturbation**: vary compliance, availability, cost, deadline and evidence-freshness objectives without granting a scoring engine implicit precedence authority;
- **AI/low-code evidence authority delta**: compare authority and evidentiary meaning before and after generated aggregation, summarization, mapping, waiver proposal or closure suggestion.

## Detection-candidate coverage

Existing detection routes remain adequate candidates and are not implementations:

- static/design-time obligation applicability and precedence graph checks, including cycle/unsatisfied-obligation analysis;
- current revision-vector and evidence-currentness/coverage qualification before a present compliance claim;
- waiver scope/expiry/revocation versus enforcement-cohort comparison;
- source/provenance/ordering/correction lineage checks for audit evidence;
- SoD assessment over the effective historical actor/role/delegation graph rather than only one instantaneous assignment;
- remediation ACK versus current governed postcondition/population validation;
- provider report scope/period/responsibility mapping against the canonical governed subject;
- explicit `PARTIAL/UNKNOWN` when population, provider or evidence coverage cannot be established;
- bounded evidence evaluation with explicit truncation/cardinality disclosure;
- AI/low-code authority-delta and evidence-lineage checks before generated governance claims are adopted.

These are `ConflictSignal` candidates only; none implies a `ConfirmedConflict` without concrete activation evidence.

## External evidence calibration

Fresh evidence was used only to calibrate portable semantics, not to infer a System Builder defect.

- NIST IR 8011 Vol. 1 Rev. 1 continues to frame continuous monitoring around testable controls and automatable tests, reinforcing the separation between desired control state, actual evidence and a qualified assessment conclusion.
- FedRAMP's 2026 continuous-vulnerability-management direction explicitly distinguishes detection, mitigation/remediation, reporting timeframes and historical records; it also notes that a draft standard is not itself applicable until formalized. This reinforces applicability/currentness and lifecycle qualification instead of introducing a new conflict family.
- FedRAMP's CSP Authorization Playbook distinguishes tracked remediation items, validated false positives/risk adjustments and later independent validation of closed items, reinforcing `acknowledged/remediated != independently validated closure`.

## Negative-space checks for this revisit

- turn absence of adverse evidence into evidence of absence;
- create circular obligation dependencies that escape existing applicability/graph conflict classification;
- let actor/role changes over time defeat separation-of-duty without an authority/SoD signal;
- produce a stronger aggregate compliance conclusion from a weaker or narrower evidence cut;
- treat provider report syntax/scope labels as canonical compliance truth after responsibility/scope subtraction;
- close a finding from remediation acknowledgement while residual/offline populations remain unvalidated;
- coerce `ABSENT/null/default`, `PARTIAL` or `UNKNOWN` into compliant success;
- discard audit-order/provenance uncertainty while preserving a complete-looking chronology;
- let evidence pressure silently shrink coverage while preserving an unqualified PASS;
- let human instructions or objective optimization select control precedence without owner-qualified authority;
- let AI/low-code fabricate evidence, widen exception/control scope or strengthen confidence beyond its source evidence/authority.

This is not a claim that bugs cannot exist. It means the material conflict/failure classes exercised here are already catalogued, ownerable and associated with detection/remediation routes.
