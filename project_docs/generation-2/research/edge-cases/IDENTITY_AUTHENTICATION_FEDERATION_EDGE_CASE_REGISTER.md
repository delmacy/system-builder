## Material edge cases

### G2-EDGE-IDENTITY-001 — Ambiguous merge/split/link silently rewrites canonical identity
Preconditions: two external identities correlate to one subject, or one historical identity is proposed for split/merge. Trigger: heuristic/email/provider match or concurrent link operation. Expected safe behavior: preserve candidate mappings, lineage and ambiguity; require qualified authorized adoption. Forbidden: first-match auto-merge or provider ID becoming canonical. Disposition: `INCONCLUSIVE` until mapping evidence/authority qualifies. Owners: Identity semantic owner + Authorization for adoption authority. Evidence/currentness: mapping revision, provenance, proofing evidence, current authority. Recovery: unlink/supersede with lineage; reconcile affected sessions/effects. Blast radius: subject through enterprise if privileged identity. Severity: CRITICAL. Misuse: plausible/adversarial. Proof: ambiguous correlations cannot silently unify/split canonical identity.

### G2-EDGE-IDENTITY-002 — Session/token outlives material authority or identity revision
Preconditions: valid session spans Role/Station/policy/trust/identity disablement or assurance revision. Trigger: later request using still-cryptographically-valid evidence. Expected: actuation-time qualification against required currentness/revisions; authentication never freezes authorization. Forbidden: session validity treated as permission continuity. Disposition: stale evidence becomes insufficient/`INCONCLUSIVE` or re-auth required as policy dictates. Owners: Identity for session evidence; Authorization/Organization for authority. Recovery: revoke/drain/re-auth and re-evaluate authority. Blast radius: protected resources. Severity: CRITICAL. Misuse: likely. Proof: stale session cannot preserve withdrawn authority.

### G2-EDGE-IDENTITY-003 — Concurrent disable/revoke/login creates residual authenticated cohort
Preconditions: login/refresh races disable, credential revocation or provider cutover. Trigger: issuance accepted on one path while revocation becomes current on another. Expected: record producing revisions and residual cohort; convergence is not inferred from control-plane ACK. Forbidden: report revocation complete while stale sessions remain effective without disposition. Effect: `PARTIAL/UNKNOWN` until cohort reconciliation. Owners: Identity + provider realization owner. Recovery: enumerate/drain/revoke/requalify residual sessions. Blast radius: subject/system. Severity: HIGH. Misuse: plausible. Proof: revocation completion requires qualified effective-state evidence.

### G2-EDGE-IDENTITY-004 — Federation metadata/key rotation crosses stale verifier caches
Preconditions: issuer metadata/JWKS/trust relationship changes while consumers cache old material. Trigger: assertion accepted/rejected by divergent verifier epochs. Expected: currentness horizon and trust/binding revision remain explicit; unavailable evidence cannot be silently extended. Forbidden: cryptographic validity under stale material treated as current federation qualification. Disposition: qualified accept/reject/`INCONCLUSIVE` by policy. Owners: Identity/Federation + Enterprise Trust/PKI where trust material applies. Recovery: refresh/requalify and disposition assertions produced/accepted during ambiguity. Blast radius: federation/system. Severity: HIGH. Misuse: plausible. Proof: verifier epoch divergence is observable and cannot silently widen acceptance.

### G2-EDGE-IDENTITY-005 — Provider substitution preserves protocol name but changes subject/assurance semantics
Preconditions: OIDC/SAML/LDAP/SCIM provider replacement or coexistence. Trigger: same feature label with different subject namespace, MFA/assurance, revocation/session or group semantics. Expected: support is qualified by semantic vector; canonical mappings requalified; provider IDs remain non-canonical. Forbidden: protocol-name equality proving substitutability. Disposition: unsupported dimensions block or become explicit degraded/unknown state. Owners: Identity + Provider/Binding + Standards/API Contracts. Recovery: bounded migration with residual cohort drainage. Blast radius: system/enterprise. Severity: CRITICAL. Misuse: plausible. Proof: substitution corpus detects semantic divergence before authoritative cutover.

### G2-EDGE-IDENTITY-006 — Offline/intermittent authentication silently extends stale evidence
Preconditions: disconnected runtime retains local identity/session closure. Trigger: upstream IdP/trust/policy currentness expires while connectivity is absent. Expected: retained closure has explicit horizon/scope; privileged claims fail closed or become `INCONCLUSIVE` after horizon. Forbidden: indefinite offline extension or reconnect declaring convergence before requalification. Owners: Identity + Authorization/Policy. Recovery: reconnect, refresh/revoke/requalify and reconcile actions taken under retained closure. Blast radius: station/system. Severity: HIGH. Misuse: plausible. Proof: offline horizon expiry cannot be bypassed by cache/restart/clock handling.

### G2-EDGE-IDENTITY-007 — Recovery/account reset or AI/low-code identity composition amplifies authority
Preconditions: reset/recovery/linking primitives are individually permitted, or AI/low-code can compose identity attributes and actions. Trigger: recovery path bypasses assurance/SoD, or generated composition links a privileged identity / treats IdP role claim as local Role. Expected: recovery and composition remain inside current authority and semantic ownership; identity attributes are evidence, not grants. Forbidden: self-approval, confused deputy, role/group claim adoption, or generated identity merge without owner authority. Owners: Identity + Authorization + AGWS/AI. Recovery: quarantine/revoke affected mapping/session and human reconciliation where needed. Blast radius: enterprise. Severity: CRITICAL. Misuse: adversarial/plausible. Proof: composition cannot create stronger identity/authority claim than independently qualified inputs.

## Reusable processual / semantic ConflictPatterns

### G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-IDENTITY-MAPPING-001 — Locally valid identity mappings are globally non-functional or contradictory
Family: semantic ownership / data / provider / version. Activation: two current provider mappings, aliases or merge/split revisions independently validate yet resolve the same external subject to incompatible canonical identities or vice versa. Incompatible claims: canonical subject ownership and lineage. Why local validation misses: each binding is valid in its provider namespace. Detection: static uniqueness/lineage checks plus pre-execution mapping-revision intersection and runtime collision signal. Owners: Identity; Provider/Binding realization owner. Severity: CRITICAL; confidence: strongly supported; detectability: static + pre-execution + runtime; blast radius: subject/system; reversibility: migration required; time-to-harm: immediate; misuse: plausible; currentness: current Planning-A/B evidence. False positives: legitimate aliases/multi-provider bindings. Future remediation: require explicit owner selection/adoption or human reconciliation; preserve lineage. Proof: no ambiguous mapping silently resolves canonical identity. Status: MATERIAL / CATALOGUED.

### G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-AUTHENTICATION-CURRENTNESS-001 — Authentication evidence and consuming authority context are individually valid but revision-incompatible
Family: temporal / authority / version. Activation: session/assertion remains valid while Role/Station/policy/trust/identity state materially changes. Incompatible claims: authenticated-current versus authority/currentness prerequisites. Local miss: token/session validator need not own authorization revisions. Detection: pre-execution material revision/currentness qualification; runtime residual-session signal. Owners: Identity + Authorization/Organization. Severity: CRITICAL; confidence: strongly supported; detectability: pre-execution/runtime; blast radius: protected resource/system; reversibility: bounded revocation plus possible compensation; time-to-harm: immediate; misuse: likely; currentness: current. False positives: policies explicitly allowing bounded session continuity. Future remediation: re-evaluate required currentness/authority; require re-auth when policy requires. Proof: authentication cannot freeze withdrawn authority. Status: MATERIAL / CATALOGUED.

### G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-FEDERATION-COEXISTENCE-001 — Old and new federation epochs are each locally valid but jointly ambiguous
Family: provider / version / migration / trust. Activation: issuer/provider/key/mapping migration leaves old and new assertions, caches or sessions active. Incompatible claims: which issuer/binding/mapping/trust revision is authoritative for new and in-flight evidence. Local miss: each epoch validates under its own configuration. Detection: residual-cohort inventory, revision-vector comparison, post-effect audit of accepted assertions. Owners: Identity/Federation + Provider/Binding + Trust where applicable. Severity: HIGH; confidence: strongly supported; detectability: pre-execution/runtime/audit; blast radius: system/enterprise; reversibility: bounded migration; time-to-harm: immediate/latent; misuse: plausible; currentness: current. False positives: explicitly governed coexistence windows. Future remediation: pin/requalify/drain cohorts and make coexistence disposition explicit. Proof: cutover cannot claim convergence while undispositioned old epochs can authenticate. Status: MATERIAL / CATALOGUED.

### G2-CONFLICT-PATTERN-RECOVERY-IDENTITY-AUTHORITY-001 — Recovery correctness conflicts with identity/authority safety
Family: recovery / authority / human-procedure / AI-low-code. Activation: reset/recovery restores access using stale proof, bypasses SoD, or composes valid recovery primitives into stronger access. Incompatible claims: availability/account recovery versus assurance/current authority constraints. Local miss: each recovery step may be valid independently. Detection: pre-execution SoD/authority/assurance check; runtime anomaly and post-effect lineage audit. Owners: Identity + Authorization/Policy; AGWS/AI for generated composition surface. Severity: CRITICAL; confidence: supported; detectability: pre-execution/runtime/audit; blast radius: subject/enterprise; reversibility: potentially migration/incident response; time-to-harm: immediate; misuse: adversarial; currentness: current. False positives: explicitly authorized emergency/break-glass procedures with bounded evidence. Future remediation: require explicit exception evidence/human reconciliation, revoke/quarantine unsafe effects. Proof: recovery cannot silently lower assurance or amplify authority. Status: MATERIAL / CATALOGUED.
