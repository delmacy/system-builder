7. **On-prem/internal rates:** OpenCost explicitly permits custom on-prem pricing and provider overrides. Existing `RateBookOrCostModelRevision` primitive is sufficient.
8. **Provider substitution/currentness:** different provider schemas, allocation availability and custom pricing mechanics continue to require re-normalization/requalification. No universal support equivalence was found.
9. **Disconnected/offline horizon:** no representative establishes timeless validity for economic evidence. Existing evidence/currentness-horizon and PARTIAL/INCONCLUSIVE rules remain sufficient; disconnected Stations may consume bounded cached evidence but cannot silently manufacture freshness.
