# Governance / Compliance / Audit — Revisit 03

## Research question
How should Generation 2 preserve governance semantics across framework tailoring/mapping, control assessment, evidence packaging, retention/integrity, exception expiry and remediation without letting compliance tooling duplicate business-domain truth or acquire execution authority?

## Representatives and evidence
1. **NIST OSCAL Control/Profile/Mapping/Assessment Results** — machine-readable catalogs/profiles, transparent tailoring, explicit many-to-many control mapping, observations/findings/risks and assessment results. Sources: https://pages.nist.gov/OSCAL/learn/concepts/layer/control/ ; https://pages.nist.gov/OSCAL/learn/concepts/layer/control/mapping/ ; https://pages.nist.gov/OSCAL/learn/concepts/layer/assessment/assessment-results/
2. **AWS Audit Manager** — evidence collection with source-dependent frequency, explicit `inconclusive` when automated evaluation is unavailable, manually curated assessment reports, report-integrity digest, and separation between collected evidence and compliance judgment. Sources: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/audit-manager/latest/userguide/how-evidence-is-collected.html ; https://docs.aws.amazon.com/audit-manager/latest/userguide/assessment-reports.html
3. **Azure Policy exemptions** — waiver versus mitigated categories, assignment-bound applicability, expiry without historical deletion, controlled selectors. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/policy/concepts/exemption-structure
4. **Microsoft Purview Audit** — retention is policy-driven and precedence/licensing-qualified rather than intrinsic to an audit event. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/audit-log-retention-policies
5. Prior OPA/Sentinel/CloudTrail evidence remains authoritative for policy realization, decision logs, override authority and qualified integrity coverage.

## Architectural primitives
Generation 2 should preserve distinct identities for `GovernanceIntent`, `ControlDefinition`, `ControlProfile/Tailoring`, `ControlMapping`, `ControlAssignment`, `EnforcementRealization`, `ControlEvaluation`, `AssessmentOccurrence`, `EvidenceObservation`, `EvidenceSelection`, `EvidencePackage`, `IntegrityVerification`, `Exception/Waiver`, `RemediationProposal`, `RemediationAuthorization`, `RemediationAttempt` and `RetentionDisposition`.

A framework mapping is not a copied control. It is a revisioned relation between authoritative control semantics. OSCAL explicitly models mappings without restating source/target content; this is the strongest evidence so far that cross-framework compliance should reference canonical control identities and mapping relations rather than fork requirements into parallel domain truth.

Evidence collection, evidence selection for a report, integrity of the selected report and compliance judgment are separate. AWS Audit Manager can collect continuously, mark evidence `inconclusive`, then let an audit owner deliberately select evidence into a report whose archive integrity is separately verifiable. Therefore `evidence exists` ≠ `evidence selected` ≠ `package integrity verified` ≠ `control effective`.

## Lifecycle / versioning / failure semantics
Independent revisions include control/catalog, profile/tailoring, mapping, assignment/applicability, enforcement realization, collector/topology, evidence observation, evidence-selection manifest, evidence package, integrity mechanism/trust root, exception, retention policy and assessment judgment.

Required states include at least `COMPLIANT`, `NON_COMPLIANT`, `EXEMPT`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `STALE_EVIDENCE`, `COVERAGE_INSUFFICIENT`, `COLLECTION_FAILED`, `INTEGRITY_UNVERIFIED`, `RETENTION_UNAVAILABLE`, `REMEDIATION_NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `REMEDIATION_FAILED` and `UNKNOWN` where applicable.

Exception expiry is a new effective-governance state, not deletion. Azure preserves the exemption object after `expiresOn` while ceasing to treat the resource as exempt. Historical lineage therefore must retain the decision while effective applicability changes.

Retention is also contextual. Purview shows that retention depends on policy priority and entitlement/licensing context. Generation 2 must not infer audit durability from event existence alone; a retention claim requires policy revision, subject/scope, effective precedence, storage realization and observed preservation evidence.

## Governance / authority boundaries
Assessment authority and remediation authority must remain non-amplifying and separately referenced. A control assessor may conclude `NON_COMPLIANT` without permission to change the governed system. A remediation proposal may be generated by AI/AGWS or an external governance provider, but execution requires independent `RemediationAuthorization` bound to exact target revision/scope and permitted action.

Enterprise → Station → Role → Person inheritance remains non-amplifying. Stations may receive tailored profiles, assignments and delegated exception authority only within explicit grants. A lower layer can add stricter local controls, but cannot weaken superior invariants merely by creating a local profile or mapping.

Extension/provider admission and revocation should consume governance decisions, but marketplace/provider metadata does not become control authority. Governance may judge or authorize an admission/revocation transition; execution remains with the owning extension/provider/runtime authority.

## Portability / offline closure
Qualified local governance closure must include the effective control/profile/mapping/assignment revisions, authority grants, active exceptions with expiry semantics, trust roots/integrity rules, evidence schemas, local collectors/evaluators needed for the declared profile, retention/disposition rules required during disconnection, and a reconciliation contract for later evidence upload. Offline operation is not governance-blind operation.

## Product-specific mechanism vs universal primitive
Provider-specific: OSCAL serialization details; Audit Manager assessment UI/report archive structure; Azure exemption resource shape; Purview licensing tiers/retention products; OPA/Sentinel wire vocabularies.

Universal: control/profile/mapping identity; applicability; assignment; evidence observation/selection/package separation; evidence qualification; integrity verification; exception lifecycle; retention/disposition claim; assessment/remediation authority separation; delegated governance; qualified local closure.

## Convergent findings
- Framework interoperability is a mapping problem, not a duplicate-control problem.
- Evidence qualification and evidence packaging are independently revisioned from source observations.
- Integrity proves selected package bytes, not semantic completeness or control effectiveness.
- Retention is policy/context qualified, not an intrinsic event property.
- Exception history survives expiry while effective authority changes.
- Assessment does not confer remediation authority.

## Reconciliation hypotheses
- **GENERALIZE** canonical control/profile/mapping relations and preserve source-framework lineage.
- **HARDEN** evidence contracts with source/revision/scope/window/freshness/coverage/trust plus explicit selection/package manifests.
- **GENERALIZE** retention/disposition as a governed policy effect with observed preservation evidence.
- **HARDEN** `AssessmentAuthorityRef != RemediationAuthorityRef` and require target-revision-bound remediation authorization.
- **PROVIDERIZE** catalogs, policy engines, audit stores, report builders and retention mechanisms behind portable governance contracts.
- **DO_NOT_BUILD** framework-specific duplicated business semantics or an all-powerful compliance plane that can mutate canonical domains by itself.

## Repository-validation questions
1. Can existing evidence contracts distinguish observation, selection, package and verification identity?
2. Is there a first-class mapping relation between requirements/controls without duplicating canonical semantic content?
3. Can retention/disposition claims bind policy revision, precedence, subject/scope and observed preservation?
4. Are assessment/reconciliation authority and remediation/execution authority distinct in existing contracts?
5. Can exception expiry change effective applicability while preserving historical identity?
6. Can Station-local governance continue evaluating/recording evidence offline and later reconcile without central Builder authority?

## Symbiotic Proof
Define one canonical control, tailor it through a profile, map it to a second external framework without copying the control text, assign it at Enterprise and specialize applicability at Station. Produce one compliant observation, one inconclusive collection gap, one temporary waiver that expires, and one non-compliant assessment. Curate a subset into an evidence package, verify package integrity, prove that integrity does not upgrade inconclusive evidence to compliant, and attempt AI-generated remediation that is rejected until a separate target-bound remediation authorization exists. Disconnect the Station and prove local evaluation/evidence retention/reconciliation under its qualified closure profile.

## Stable findings
- **G2-FINDING-GCA-23 — Cross-Framework Compliance Must Use Revisioned Control Mapping Relations, Not Duplicated Canonical Semantics.** Tailoring and many-to-many mapping preserve authoritative-source lineage and support change-impact analysis without creating parallel business truth.
- **G2-FINDING-GCA-24 — Evidence Observation, Evidence Selection, Evidence Package and Integrity Verification Are Distinct Identities.** A curated, integrity-valid report can still be incomplete or semantically inconclusive.
- **G2-FINDING-GCA-25 — Compliance Evidence Requires Explicit `INCONCLUSIVE` / Coverage-Failure Semantics; Collection Presence Does Not Imply Evaluability.** Automated evidence gaps must remain first-class uncertainty.
- **G2-FINDING-GCA-26 — Audit Retention Is a Policy- and Context-Qualified Effect Requiring Its Own Revision and Preservation Evidence.** Event existence alone does not prove required retention.
- **G2-FINDING-GCA-27 — Exception Expiry Changes Effective Applicability Without Deleting Historical Authorization Lineage.** Waiver/mitigation identity and effective status must remain separately queryable.
- **G2-FINDING-GCA-28 — Assessment Authority and Remediation Authority Must Be Constitutionally Separate and Target-Revision-Bound.** Governance can detect, assess and propose without gaining execution authority.
- **G2-FINDING-GCA-29 — Qualified Local Governance Closure Must Include Control/Profile/Mapping/Authority/Exception/Trust/Retention Inputs Needed for Offline Evaluation and Later Reconciliation.** Offline autonomy cannot silently suspend governance.

## Value / risk / priority
Value is foundational: the architecture can support multiple regulations/frameworks without semantic duplication and can prove what evidence was actually available, selected, retained and authorized. Main risk is Governance absorbing Authorization, Records Management, Storage or remediation execution. Priority remains high for synthesis and the future negative-space gate.
