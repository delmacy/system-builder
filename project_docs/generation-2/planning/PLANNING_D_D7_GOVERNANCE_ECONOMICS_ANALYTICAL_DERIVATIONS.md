## 6. Governance / Compliance / Audit

Governance migrates by separating policy definition/revision, applicability, decision occurrence, enforcement realization, exception/waiver, evidence and assessment.

Existing documents, approvals, tickets, spreadsheets, audit exports and operator procedures enter as evidence/claims with provenance/currentness. They are not promoted to canonical policy because they are widely used.

Policy revisions are effective-dated. Historical decisions retain the policy/revision and evidence available when made. A newer policy does not rewrite historical decision truth. Exceptions and waivers require explicit scope, authority, validity horizon and revocation/supersession lineage.

Compliance assessment is derived from qualified evidence and control applicability. `no finding != compliant`; missing evidence/population remains visible. Provider certifications/attestations are evidence with scope/currentness, not automatic canonical compliance.

Audit records preserve occurrence and correction lineage; correction appends/supersedes rather than silently rewriting history.

## 7. Privacy / Data Governance / Retention / Legal Hold / Residency

Data classification, purpose/legal-basis claims, retention rules, holds, residency constraints and disposition decisions remain distinct semantic owners/revisions.

Migration inventories canonical datasets plus copies, replicas, caches, backups, exports, analytics extracts, provider residuals and offline Station populations. A canonical deletion request does not imply all-copy destruction. Legal hold may intentionally prevent destruction without making a retained copy current or authoritative.

Retention/hold/residency policies are effective-dated and population-scoped. Provider region labels and storage locations are realization evidence, not policy satisfaction by themselves. Cross-border/provider migration requires explicit support qualification and residual-copy drainage.

Privacy transformations such as masking/tokenization/aggregation retain transformation revision and lossiness/irreversibility metadata. Derived data does not escape original governance merely because identifiers were transformed unless the applicable policy/assessment explicitly establishes that result.

## 8. Commercial / Monetization / Entitlements

Commercial migration preserves separate identities/revisions for product, offer, plan, price, contract/subscription, entitlement, meter, usage evidence, rating, charge, invoice and payment/settlement.

Historical pricing/rating is effective-dated and revision-pinned. Currency, minor units, rounding, proration, discounts/taxes and ordering rules are explicit inputs to reproducible calculation traces.

Usage migration distinguishes observed usage from qualified usage. Missing or stale evidence cannot become zero usage by default. Corrections/rerating supersede prior calculations with lineage rather than mutating historical evidence.

Entitlement is a commercial grant/limit and cannot amplify Authorization. Revocation/cancellation/deprovision requires residual cohort drainage across entitlement caches, open periods, delayed usage, pending invoices/payments/refunds/disputes, callbacks and offline snapshots.

Payment/provider acknowledgements remain attempts/evidence. Ambiguous remote settlement remains UNKNOWN and reconciles before retry unless operation/provider/scope/horizon-specific duplicate safety is proven.

## 9. Technology Economic Governance / FinOps

FinOps owns normalized technology-economic evidence and decisions, not provider billing truth or customer-commercial truth.

Migration separates provider invoice/usage records, normalized cost evidence, allocation rules, shared-cost pools, budgets, forecasts, commitments, unit economics, showback/chargeback and commercial billing.

Normalization preserves currency/unit, FX/rate source and effective time, provider pricing revision, allocation revision, source population and correction lineage. Late provider corrections produce superseding economic evidence; they do not silently rewrite prior reports.

Shared-cost allocation must conserve the qualified source amount subject to explicit rounding/residual policy. Unallocated/unknown populations remain visible. Provider migration/coexistence preserves overlapping billing windows and residual economic cohorts until reconciled.

Relative operational complexity per capability may be modeled only from auditable architectural/operational facts and as a bounded multidimensional vector/profile. It is not a universal scalar quality score and cannot silently become price, entitlement, performance, risk or implementation complexity. Rating/pricing policy may consume that profile explicitly with revisioned rules.

## 10. Mathematical and analytical derivations

Mathematical mechanics remain cross-cutting/providerizable while semantics stay portable. Every expression/rule/model has identity/revision, typed inputs, dimensions/units, domain/preconditions, rounding/precision, uncertainty handling, output semantic kind and provenance.

Vector semantics preserve basis/dimension/order and do not collapse to scalar without an explicit projection rule. Unit conversion is a typed transformation, not string relabeling. Temporal calculations preserve event/effective/observation/processing times and declared windows.

Uncertainty remains first-class; UNKNOWN/PARTIAL/INCONCLUSIVE inputs cannot be silently coerced to precise values. Aggregation preserves population/coverage. Transformation chains preserve lineage and may not strengthen evidence, authority or certainty.

Graph transformations are revisioned, owner-preserving and non-strengthening. Derived graph edges cannot manufacture canonical authority/source-of-truth edges.

Causality remains research-only: observational correlation, temporal precedence or model attribution cannot be promoted to causal truth without a separately admitted causal proof regime.
