Detection produces evidence/finding/contradiction state. It does **not** automatically create a `ConflictInstance`, alter canonical policy or remediate the system.

## 9. Queueing / flow / capacity and finite drainage

E6 acceptance tracks asynchronous queues for audit/evidence ingestion, retention/disposition, hold propagation, usage qualification, rating, invoice/payment reconciliation, provider cost import, allocation, analytical recomputation and correction propagation.

For every applicable queue, the proof must identify:

- expected arrival rate and service/drain capacity;
- peak/burst and reconnect/correction-wave assumptions;
- queue depth and **oldest age**;
- retry/redrive/fan-out amplification;
- provider quota/rate limit and dependency health;
- prioritization/starvation behavior;
- blocked-owner age;
- capacity headroom;
- terminal/drain/reconciliation evidence.

A shallow queue or low average utilization does not prove finite drainage. A closure claim requires either demonstrated drainability under the declared workload envelope or an explicit bounded steady-state policy with owner acceptance.

## 10. Operability Elicitation Lens — E6 carry-forward

For each governance rule, privacy population, meter/rating path, provider cost source, allocation/model and analytical derivation, elicitation must answer at least:

- **Como saberemos que está funcionando?** What positive evidence and currentness horizon establish the claim?
- **Como saberemos que está degradado?** Which lag, stale evidence, coverage loss, mismatch, queue age, error or uncertainty thresholds matter?
- **Quem é responsável?** Who owns the semantic rule, evidence source, on-call response, exception and reconciliation?
- **Que evidência precisamos?** Which source, lineage, population, revision and retention are required?
- **Qual estado pode permanecer UNKNOWN?** For how long, with what containment and downstream blocking?
- **Qual perda/atraso é aceitável?** Usage lag, evidence lag, invoice delay, hold propagation, correction delay, cost import latency and analytical staleness must be explicit.
- **Como recuperar?** Rebuild, rerate, recompute, restore, correction, roll-forward, compensation or manual recovery route.
- **Como reconciliar?** Source-to-derived, provider-to-normalized, measured-to-qualified, invoiced-to-settled and local-to-Fleet reconciliation ownership.
- **Como validar depois de mudança/deploy?** Post-change proof must compare revisions, residual cohorts, queue/currentness and protected negative invariants.

Additional mandatory questions cover SLO/SLA, throughput, peak/burst, latency, queue/backlog, retry/idempotency, timeout/UNKNOWN, dependency health, evidence retention, alert thresholds, escalation, maintenance windows, provider quotas, degraded/offline operation, rollback/roll-forward, capacity headroom, cost/usage, audit and incident response.

Operational elicitation metadata is not pricing authority, compliance authority or analytics authority. It is qualified input to the respective semantic owner and to Production Readiness Coverage.

## 13. Local / Station / Fleet and Physical / Peripheral boundaries
