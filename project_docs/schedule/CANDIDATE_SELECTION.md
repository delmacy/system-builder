# Sprint Candidate Selection

Candidate selection operates inside the rolling-wave policy; it does not itself commit work.

From the dependency-safe eligible set, prefer work that contributes to one coherent Sprint Goal and growing integrated proof. Consider critical-path unblockers, risk reduction, contract stabilization, vertical integration value and review/integration capacity.

Before promotion, revalidate fresh `main`, predecessor outputs, affected contracts/interfaces, current repository memory, risks, stop conditions and the Work Package transition allowed by `SPRINT_GENERATION_POLICY.md`/`SPRINT_MODE.md`.

Do not maximize task count. A smaller bounded Sprint that stabilizes a shared contract or proves an integrated slice may be more valuable than a larger collection of isolated TASKs.

READY, candidate and FORECAST states are planning classifications only. Only an explicitly materialized/COMMITTED Sprint and its declared TASK set may execute. Do not silently pull adjacent debt, successor scope or optional Construction work into the active Sprint.
