# Definition of Schedule Ready

A planning horizon is Schedule Ready when:
- target milestone/increment is explicit;
- relevant WPs have complete Dictionary entries;
- activities/tasks are estimable;
- blocking DAG edges and gates are explicit;
- topological order is valid;
- critical path/float can be calculated from estimates;
- resource/review/CI constraints are known;
- READY queue can be produced mechanically;
- sprint candidate can be assembled without architectural invention.

This gate is stricter than Scope Ready and precedes actual sprint instantiation.
