# Change Impact on DAG

When a change is proposed, identify affected requirement/WBS/WP, direct successors, transitive successors, interfaces/contracts, milestone gates, estimates/float and already committed sprint work.

Approved scope change may add/remove DAG nodes or edges through change control. Decomposition correction within existing scope may refine nodes/edges without redefining business scope, but must remain traceable.

A dependency discovered during execution is recorded immediately. Block affected successors; do not block unrelated branches. Recompute readiness and critical path for the planning horizon.
