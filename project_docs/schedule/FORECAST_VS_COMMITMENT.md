# Forecast vs Commitment

A future sprint package may be forecast without being committed.

Forecast means: likely READY work based on current DAG, estimates and capacity. It can change after predecessor evidence/review.

Commitment means: sprint has started; Goal and committed tasks are controlled until closure.

This distinction lets the project prepare several likely future sprints without pretending their detailed contents are immutable. It also lets dependent work remain visible while correctly BLOCKED.
