# Increment Policy

An increment is a coherent integrated capability that advances a milestone and can be demonstrated/validated. It may span several sprints.

Plan increments before exact sprint numbering. A large capability such as Identity/Auth may require several construction sprints, while an independent Capability Registry increment can progress without waiting for that entire chain.

A successor increment can be forecast while its predecessor is active. Its blocked tasks remain uncommitted; independent READY tasks may be selected earlier.

Every increment ends with integration evidence and updates successor readiness. Periodic system review examines interactions among several increments and accumulated debt.
