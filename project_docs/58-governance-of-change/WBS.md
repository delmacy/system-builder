# WBS — 58 Governance of Change

## 58.0 Governance of Change
### 58.1 Change classification
- **58.1.1** Formalizar L1 Local, L2 Module, L3 Contract e L4 Architecture.
- **58.1.2** Definir triggers de ADR/security/data/migration review.
- **58.1.3** Definir authority/required reviewers por nível/risco.
### 58.2 Change evaluation
- **58.2.1** Executar impact/compatibility/risk analysis proporcional.
- **58.2.2** Definir migration, deprecation, rollout e rollback quando necessários.
- **58.2.3** Registrar rationale, evidence e exceptions.
### 58.3 Change lifecycle
- **58.3.1** Ligar approval a tasks/releases/deployments correspondentes.
- **58.3.2** Tratar emergency changes com follow-up obrigatório.
- **58.3.3** Verificar resultado pós-mudança e fechar ChangeRecord.
