## Deductions — research only

### D1 — Surface identity is not action identity

Candidate chain:

`gesture/control → capability/action resolution → admitted canonical mutation → consequence/result → projection convergence/currentness`.

A button, menu item, command-palette entry or Tool affordance may invoke the same semantic action without duplicating action identity. Placement/style changes cannot silently alter capability, permission or domain meaning.

### D2 — Consequences belong after admitted mutation

A successful admitted action may affect multiple semantic objects/relations and may emit correlated result/provenance. Station may present or coordinate the result, but presentation does not become mutation or business-history authority.

### D3 — Accepted is not visibly current

Keep distinct:
1. command/action accepted;
2. canonical state current at a known revision;
3. affected projections visibly converged to that revision.

This sharpens Matrix 01 stale/convergence proofs rather than duplicating them.

### D4 — Application richness does not prove Studio

Ontology-backed operational applications can combine many views, actions and tools without requiring a new canonical-data species. No evidence here justifies `C11` or `Studio = collection<Tool>`.

Provisional C10 discriminator remains:
`shared working artifact/context + coordinated multi-Tool structural authoring + dependency/consequence convergence + version/currentness lifecycle + materialization/reopen`.

Status: `unproven-gap`.
