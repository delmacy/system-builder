## Exit conclusion

R7 has completed its bounded research purpose. It does not authorize Construction or a specialized Studio.

The evidence supports C9 Application as a lifecycle/assembly boundary over already-defined contributions. Application identity, presentation, launch policy and contribution assembly remain distinct from ComponentRegistry ownership and from Core/business authority.

C10 Studio is not promoted merely by richer UI, more Tools, a saved workspace, editing capability, branding, or a domain-specific name. The only remaining promotable hypothesis is a reusable semantic delta that C9 + configuration cannot express, provisionally:

`shared working artifact/context + coordinated multi-Tool structural authoring + version/currentness lifecycle + cross-Tool consequence convergence`.

R7B must challenge/falsify this hypothesis across independent engineering-workbench families before synthesis.

## Proof Grammar carry-forward

Coverage vocabulary remains `proven | failed | unproven-gap | not-applicable`. Absence of evidence is never PASS. Lower-level proofs are inherited only when their preconditions remain unchanged; higher levels prove only their semantic delta.

Exact-head repository gates prove repository integrity for the head on which they ran. They do not prove untested C9/C10 semantics and do not create product authority.

Machine conformance and human product acceptance remain distinct.

## Proven / reusable evidence

Existing Station runtime evidence is reusable for bounded C9 properties including identity/presentation separation, duplicate/reference rejection, provider-neutral launch projection, launch-policy behavior independent from application identity, and rejection of authority-shaped manifest fields.

Existing Core/Station contracts are reusable/projection candidates for target/currentness, expected revision, command/conditions/authority/result separation, accepted-versus-effective result semantics, partial/unknown/stale/reconcile-required outcomes, retry qualification, provenance, and Station -> Gateway -> Core ownership boundaries.

These proofs must not be strengthened beyond their actual scope.
