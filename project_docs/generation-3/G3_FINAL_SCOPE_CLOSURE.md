## Closure question

The closure program asked whether any relevant class of enterprise system, process, organization, information, integration, authority, execution or operation could not be expressed by composition of the frozen G3 structures.

No such class was demonstrated. Heterogeneous adversarial cases repeatedly refined qualifications and proof obligations rather than requiring a new primitive, capability family, sector kernel exception or macrofront.

## Coverage inventory

All 21 frozen fronts listed in `README.md` are covered by the Minimal Primitive Basis, eight capability families, conflict/boundary matrix, proof-obligation matrix and implementation-independent target architecture. Fronts are research coverage categories, not a physical module decomposition.

## Five final sufficiency proofs

- **Representational Sufficiency — SATURATED/PASS.** Entities, concepts, resources and relations from heterogeneous systems are representable through canonical/definition/occurrence/realization identities and typed qualified relations without sector-specific kernel primitives.
- **Behavioral Sufficiency — SATURATED/PASS.** Events, actions, decisions, rules, calculations, states, transitions, retries, compensation, handoffs, timers and conditional processes compose from existing structures.
- **Governance Sufficiency — SATURATED/PASS.** Authority, conditional and multiple approvals, delegation, escalation, veto, SoD, obligations, exceptions/waivers, evidence and effective periods compose without hardcoded process primitives.
- **Compositional Sufficiency — SATURATED/PASS.** Assumption/guarantee qualification and cross-owner conflict proofs preserve system correctness when individually valid capabilities are combined.
- **Evolutionary Sufficiency — SATURATED/PASS.** Versioning, clone, migration, provider/technology replacement, integration, rollback, module removal and decommission are representable without losing semantic identity, lineage, authority, evidence or history.

## Cross-domain final reconciliation

Stress cases included telecom/SIP/endpoints/sessions/protocols; manufacturing/machines/lots/sensors/production; administrative/professional services; hospitality; maintenance/physical assets; logistics; retail; distributed/multiunit organizations; third parties/outsourcing; document-heavy operation; intermittent/offline connectivity; long human processes; intense automation; brownfield/federation; and regulated operation.

These cases remain adversarial proofs only. No `Telecom`, `Manufacturing`, `Hotel`, `Finance` or other sector front is created.

## Portability closure

All seven axes are structurally covered: data portability; semantic portability; definition portability; integration/provider portability; operational autonomy; evolutionary portability; evidence/history portability. Dependencies may exist and migrations may be expensive, but implementation language, provider, framework and physical topology do not define canonical semantic identity.

## Saturation rationale

Saturation is evidence-based rather than cycle-count based. Late standards/systems/failure cases and cross-domain/compositional tests ceased to alter the Minimal Primitive Basis, eight capability families, semantic-owner boundaries or implementation-independent target architecture. Late findings instead hardened existing proofs such as ACK != effect, reconcile-before-retry, cross-owner qualification, semantic continuity through migration, and safe decommission.

## Reopening policy

G3 may be reopened only if:

1. a genuinely new class of problem is structurally unrepresentable/inoperable/ungovernable;
2. technological or regulatory change invalidates a structural decision;
3. Product Proof or production demonstrates a model failure; or
4. an explicit new strategic requirement is introduced.

A new framework, provider, paper, product, protocol or industry alone is insufficient.

## Construction philosophy preserved

Future work should preserve `broad architecture up front + narrow implementation incrementally + empirical learning continuously`. Architectural closure reduces structural uncertainty; future Product Proof and production reduce empirical uncertainty. Planning should be rolling-wave/evidence-gated, with predecessor contracts/proofs and successor eligibility. `Sequential Governance != Serial Execution`.
