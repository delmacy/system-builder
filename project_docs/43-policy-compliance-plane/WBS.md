# WBS — 43 Policy & Compliance Plane

## 43.0 Policy & Compliance Plane
### 43.1 Policy/control model
- **43.1.1** Definir policy, control, requirement e applicability model.
- **43.1.2** Definir jurisdiction/organization/effective-period context.
- **43.1.3** Versionar policies e mappings para controls.
### 43.2 Enforcement e evidence
- **43.2.1** Integrar hooks em authorization, data, document e workflow.
- **43.2.2** Capturar evidence/control status sem duplicar source data.
- **43.2.3** Detectar nonconformance e missing evidence.
### 43.3 Governance
- **43.3.1** Gerenciar exception/waiver/compensating control.
- **43.3.2** Produzir compliance reports/packages.
- **43.3.3** Reavaliar aplicabilidade quando policy/jurisdiction mudar.
