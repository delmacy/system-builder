# G4 — Componentes Conformance Evidence Provenance, Retention & Freshness Research

Status: `RESEARCH_ACTIVE / NON_EXECUTABLE`
Execution authority: NONE
Date: 2026-09-23

## Purpose

Extend the G4 `Componentes` catalog/playground conformance model with an implementation-independent model for evidence provenance, retention, compaction, reproducibility and freshness horizons across:

`primitive/atomic -> compound -> module component -> tool -> application -> window -> desktop -> workspace -> system view`.

This is research only. It does not select Sigstore/Rekor, Storybook, an artifact store, database, CI provider or retention provider. It does not authorize implementation, WBS, Work Packages, Sprints or TASKs.

## Repository constraints preserved

- `Builder != Runtime`.
- `Research candidate != implementation authority`.
- `PASS != permanent truth`.
- `STALE != CURRENT`.
- `Evidence sufficient != raw artifact retained forever`.
- `Evidence metadata != canonical business truth`.
- `Evidence retention != runtime retention`.
- `Evidence provenance != semantic correctness`.
- `Compaction != semantic forgetting`.

This research builds on `G4_COMPONENTES_CONFORMANCE_EVIDENCE_INVALIDATION_RESEARCH.md`, which establishes typed invalidation, proof-class-sensitive propagation, compatibility claims and external normative/profile invalidation roots.

## External interaction-grammar evidence

1. Sigstore/Rekor demonstrates a useful separation between a large artifact and durable signed metadata: artifact digest, signature/certificate and timestamped transparency-log inclusion can support later provenance/audit without making the transparency log the artifact store. This is evidence for content-addressed evidence identity and durable witness records, not Sigstore adoption.
2. Rekor is append-only and supports retrieval/inclusion verification by artifact hash/UUID. Its sharding model shows that long-lived audit identity can survive storage partitioning/rotation. This is evidence for stable evidence references across retention tiers, not a requirement for Merkle logs.
3. Sigstore explicitly does not prove that an artifact is semantically “good”; provenance/authorship and semantic qualification remain separate. This matches the G4 rule that provenance evidence cannot manufacture conformance.
4. WCAG-EM 2.0 (published 2026-07-23) requires evaluation scope, target, relied-upon technologies, accessibility support baseline, representative sample and findings to be reported. This supports retaining evaluation context/profile identity independently of raw screenshots/traces.

## Finding 1 — Evidence has at least three retention layers

A conformance result should not be modeled as one indivisible blob.

Candidate decomposition:

```text
EvidenceRecord
  EvidenceIdentity
  EvidenceClaim
  EvidenceProvenance
  EvidenceContext
  EvidenceDisposition
  ArtifactManifest[]
  ReproductionEnvelope?
  WitnessRefs[]
  RetentionClass
  FreshnessPolicyRef
```

Retention layers:

```text
CLAIM_LEDGER
  compact durable statement of what was evaluated and concluded

PROVENANCE_ENVELOPE
  subject revisions, fixture/profile/environment identities, producer/toolchain identity,
  dependency evidence refs, artifact digests and causal lineage

RAW_EVIDENCE_ARTIFACTS
  screenshots, traces, videos, DOM/accessibility snapshots, logs, performance samples,
  serialized failure injections and other potentially large evidence
```

Invariants:

- `CLAIM_RETAINED != RAW_ARTIFACT_RETAINED`
- `RAW_ARTIFACT_DELETED != CLAIM_NEVER_EXISTED`
- `DIGEST_RETAINED != ARTIFACT_REPRODUCIBLE`
- `PROVENANCE_VALID != CONFORMANCE_PASS`
- `SIGNED != SEMANTICALLY_CORRECT`

## Finding 2 — Evidence identity should be content/revision bound, not filename bound

Candidate stable identity:

```text
EvidenceIdentity
  evidenceId
  subjectId
  subjectContractRevision
  implementationRevision?
  proofClass
  fixtureRevision
  normativeProfileRefs[]
  executionProfileId
  artifactManifestDigest
  evidenceSchemaRevision
```

Artifact references should include immutable digest plus media/schema type and logical role. Mutable paths may be convenience locators only.

`SAME_FILENAME != SAME_EVIDENCE`.
`SAME_DIGEST != SAME_CLAIM_CONTEXT`.

Two executions may produce byte-identical screenshots but different authority/currentness/profile context. Conversely, different encodings may support the same qualified observable only through an explicit equivalence/compatibility claim.

## Finding 3 — Provenance is causal lineage, not just “generated by CI”

Candidate provenance envelope:

```text
EvidenceProvenance
  producerIdentity
  producerVersion
  toolchainIdentities[]
  sourceRevision
  dependencyEvidenceRefs[]
  fixtureRevision
  normativeProfileRefs[]
  environmentFingerprint
  startedAt
  completedAt
  parentEvidenceRefs[]
  supersedesEvidenceRefs[]
  witnessRefs[]
```

The producer may be automated, manual, hybrid or third-party. Producer identity is evidence about origin; it is not semantic authority.

- `CI_PRODUCED != TRUSTED`
- `HUMAN_REVIEWED != CURRENT`
- `THIRD_PARTY_ATTESTED != SB_QUALIFIED`
- `PRODUCER_SIGNATURE != PROOF_CLASS_SATISFIED`

## Finding 4 — Freshness is claim-specific and multi-horizon

There is no universal evidence TTL. Candidate horizons:

```text
SOURCE_REVISION_HORIZON
DEPENDENCY_REVISION_HORIZON
NORMATIVE_PROFILE_HORIZON
BROWSER_AT_PROFILE_HORIZON
DEVICE_INPUT_PROFILE_HORIZON
PERFORMANCE_ENVIRONMENT_HORIZON
SECURITY_POLICY_HORIZON
MANUAL_REVIEW_HORIZON
CALENDAR_REASSESSMENT_HORIZON
```

A proof is `CURRENT` only if every horizon material to its proof class remains admissible.

Examples:

- a structural composition proof may remain current indefinitely while bound revisions remain unchanged;
- accessibility evidence can stale when the declared browser/AT support baseline or normative profile changes;
- performance evidence can stale when the execution profile or budget changes even if source does not;
- a manual usability review may have an explicit reassessment horizon even with no code diff.

Invariants:

- `YOUNG_EVIDENCE != CURRENT_EVIDENCE`
- `OLD_EVIDENCE != STALE_EVIDENCE` by age alone unless a calendar horizon is declared
- `SOURCE_CURRENT != PROFILE_CURRENT`
- `PROFILE_CURRENT != ENVIRONMENT_REPRESENTATIVE`

## Finding 5 — Freshness disposition is a vector before it is an aggregate

Candidate dimensions:

```text
sourceCurrentness
fixtureCurrentness
dependencyCurrentness
normativeCurrentness
environmentRepresentativeness
producerQualificationCurrentness
artifactAvailability
reproducibilityDisposition
```

Candidate dispositions:

```text
CURRENT
STALE
EXPIRED_BY_POLICY
SUPERSEDED
ARTIFACT_PRUNED
REPRODUCIBLE
PARTIALLY_REPRODUCIBLE
NON_REPRODUCIBLE
UNKNOWN
```

`ARTIFACT_PRUNED` does not automatically mean `STALE`: a claim may remain current while raw evidence has aged out, if policy explicitly permits the surviving provenance/claim envelope to justify that proof class. Conversely, a perfectly retained screenshot can be stale.

## Finding 6 — Retention class follows proof obligations and audit/reproduction needs

Candidate retention classes:

```text
EPHEMERAL_DEBUG
SHORT_LIVED_RAW
QUALIFICATION_WINDOW
RELEASE_BOUND
SUPERSESSION_BOUND
AUDIT_LONG_LIVED
SECURITY_OR_SAFETY_LONG_LIVED
LEGAL_OR_POLICY_BOUND
```

Each class should specify separately:

```text
claimLedgerRetention
provenanceEnvelopeRetention
rawArtifactRetention
reproductionInputsRetention
minimumWitnessRetention
redaction/privacyPolicy
```

The system must not infer “keep forever” merely because evidence once supported PASS.

`QUALIFIED != INFINITE_RETENTION`.
`PRUNABLE_RAW != PRUNABLE_LINEAGE`.

## Finding 7 — Compaction may replace raw detail only when live questions remain answerable

Candidate compaction product:

```text
EvidenceCompactionSummary
  sourceEvidenceRefs[]
  preservedClaims[]
  preservedFailures[]
  preservedUnknowns[]
  artifactDigests[]
  provenanceRootRefs[]
  normativeProfileRefs[]
  invalidationRoots[]
  retentionDecision
  compactionPolicyRevision
  producedAt
```

Compaction must preserve enough information to answer at least:

1. what subject/revision was evaluated;
2. what proof class/claim was made;
3. under what fixture/profile/environment;
4. what disposition resulted, including FAIL/PARTIAL/UNKNOWN;
5. what evidence it depended on;
6. why it later became stale/superseded, if applicable;
7. whether raw artifacts still exist and where/how their integrity is verified;
8. whether reproduction is still possible.

- `COMPACTED != PASS_ONLY`
- `COMPACTED != FAILURE_ERASED`
- `RAW_REMOVED != UNKNOWN_RELABELED`
- `SUMMARY != SYNTHETIC_NEW_PROOF`

## Finding 8 — Supersession is not deletion and does not rewrite history

Candidate lineage:

```text
Evidence A --supersededBy--> Evidence B
Evidence A --invalidatedBy--> Change C
Evidence B --requalifies--> ProofScope P
```

A newer PASS does not mutate an old FAIL into PASS. It makes the old result historical/superseded for the current claim scope.

- `NEW_PASS != OLD_FAIL_NEVER_HAPPENED`
- `SUPERSEDED != INVALID`
- `HISTORICAL != CURRENT`
- `LATEST != ONLY_RETAINED`

This supports audit without forcing every raw artifact to remain hot.

## Finding 9 — Reproducibility has levels

Candidate dispositions:

```text
FULL_REPRODUCTION
INPUT_REPLAYABLE
ENVIRONMENT_APPROXIMABLE
OBSERVABLES_ONLY_RETAINED
CLAIM_ONLY_RETAINED
NON_REPRODUCIBLE
```

A reproduction claim must identify which inputs are retained: source revision, dependency lock identity, fixture data, browser/AT/device profile, toolchain, fonts/assets where material, environment/budget profile and random/time seeds where applicable.

`SOURCE_COMMIT_AVAILABLE != RUN_REPRODUCIBLE`.
`CONTAINER_DIGEST_AVAILABLE != AT_BEHAVIOR_REPRODUCIBLE`.
`SCREENSHOT_AVAILABLE != INTERACTION_REPRODUCIBLE`.

Manual evidence may be reproducible only as a documented protocol + environment/profile + observation record; this limitation remains explicit.

## Finding 10 — Accessibility evidence needs evaluation-scope provenance

WCAG-EM 2.0 provides useful grammar: evaluation scope, conformance target, relied-upon technologies, accessibility support baseline, representative sample and findings are part of a meaningful report.

Candidate accessibility evidence envelope therefore includes:

```text
AccessibilityEvaluationContext
  productScope
  proofSubjectScope
  conformanceTarget
  normativeProfile
  technologiesReliedUpon[]
  accessibilitySupportBaseline
  representativeSampleRefs[]
  browserAtProfiles[]
  evaluator/procedure provenance
  findings[]
```

Axe-like automated output alone cannot substitute for this context at application/workspace/system-view levels.

`AUTOMATED_SCAN_RETAINED != ACCESSIBILITY_EVALUATION_REPRODUCIBLE`.

## Finding 11 — Large artifacts can move across storage tiers without changing evidence identity

Candidate tiers:

```text
HOT
WARM
COLD
ARCHIVAL
PRUNED_RAW
```

Storage tier is operational metadata, not proof disposition. Movement must preserve immutable artifact identity/digest and retrieval status.

A transparency-log-style witness is useful grammar: a durable digest/timestamp/inclusion record can remain queryable while the large artifact lives elsewhere. Rekor sharding additionally demonstrates that audit identity can survive log rotation/sharding.

- `COLD != STALE`
- `HOT != CURRENT`
- `MOVED != REPRODUCED`
- `WITNESSED_DIGEST != ARTIFACT_AVAILABLE`

## Finding 12 — Privacy/redaction is part of evidence retention

Screenshots, traces and fixtures may contain client identifiers, business data, credentials, tokens or personal data. Retention classification must therefore be purpose/data-classification aware.

Candidate artifact dispositions:

```text
SAFE_TO_RETAIN
REDACT_BEFORE_RETENTION
SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE_ONLY
RESTRICTED_ACCESS
DO_NOT_PERSIST
PURGE_AFTER_DERIVATION
```

A derived conformance claim must never imply that sensitive raw data is exempt from deletion requirements.

`EVIDENCE_VALUE != RETENTION_EXEMPTION`.
`DIGEST_RETAINED != SECRET_RETAINED`.

If deletion/redaction makes reproduction impossible, the surviving claim explicitly degrades its reproducibility disposition rather than fabricating completeness.

## Finding 13 — Failure/recovery evidence is first-class retention material

For shell/application/window/workspace behavior, recovery proofs often matter more than steady-state screenshots. Evidence manifests should support checkpoint lineage, injected fault identity, pre-fault state, post-recovery state and unresolved effects.

Examples:

```text
window owner crash
browser refresh/process loss
application activation failure
permission/currentness drift
pending/unknown effect during recovery
layout restore with stale semantic context
```

`RECOVERY_UI_RESTORED != RECOVERY_PROOF_COMPLETE`.

Proof must retain enough lineage to show that drafts/effects/currentness/authority were not silently relabeled.

## State ownership and lifecycle

Candidate evidence lifecycle:

```text
CAPTURE_REQUESTED
 -> CAPTURING
 -> PRODUCED
 -> QUALIFIED
 -> CURRENT
 -> STALE | SUPERSEDED | EXPIRED_BY_POLICY
 -> ARCHIVED
 -> RAW_PRUNED (optional)
```

Orthogonal artifact lifecycle:

```text
HOT -> WARM -> COLD -> ARCHIVAL -> PRUNED
```

Orthogonal reproducibility lifecycle:

```text
FULL -> PARTIAL -> CLAIM_ONLY -> NON_REPRODUCIBLE
```

These axes must not be collapsed.

## Accessibility / responsive requirements

Evidence lineage, freshness and retention status must be representable as structured text/table/list; no graph-only requirement is allowed. Color cannot be the sole carrier of current/stale/pruned state. Every lineage graph requires keyboard-accessible ordered/path views. Small-screen views may collapse detail progressively but must preserve disposition, cause, profile and artifact-availability semantics.

## Performance implications

The active catalog should not hydrate every historical raw artifact. Candidate query model:

```text
ACTIVE INDEX
  current claim/disposition
  compact provenance refs
  currentness horizons
  artifact availability/tier
  causal stale root

ON DEMAND
  historical lineage
  raw artifact manifests
  screenshots/traces/videos
  reproduction inputs
```

Scale by content-addressing, tiering, indexing, pagination/virtualization and summary materialization; never by silently dropping failures, unknowns or stale reasons.

`ACTIVE_INDEX_COMPACT != HISTORY_ERASED`.

## Failure/recovery implications

Evidence storage/provider outage must not transform missing retrieval into PASS/FAIL. Candidate dispositions distinguish:

```text
ARTIFACT_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE
ARTIFACT_PRUNED_BY_POLICY
ARTIFACT_INTEGRITY_FAILED
ARTIFACT_REFERENCE_UNKNOWN
```

A claim whose required evidence cannot be verified may degrade to an explicit evidence-availability/qualification disposition according to policy; it never guesses.

## Adversarial scenarios

1. PASS screenshot is retained but normative accessibility profile changes.
2. Raw trace is pruned while compact provenance/claim remains policy-sufficient.
3. Raw artifact is pruned but policy required full reproducibility: aggregate qualification must degrade.
4. Same screenshot filename is overwritten with different bytes.
5. Same artifact digest is reused under a different Client/Workspace/profile context.
6. New PASS supersedes an old FAIL; history must preserve the FAIL without making it current.
7. Storage tier migration changes URI but not digest/identity.
8. Artifact retrieval fails transiently during audit.
9. Artifact bytes are returned but digest verification fails.
10. Evidence contains client PII and must be purged while retaining non-sensitive lineage.
11. Manual screen-reader evidence outlives the declared browser/AT support baseline.
12. Performance trace is byte-retained but hardware/execution profile is no longer representative.
13. Recovery evidence shows restored layout but unresolved effect is `UNKNOWN`.
14. Compaction accidentally drops `PARTIAL`/`UNKNOWN` child results and produces aggregate PASS.
15. Producer/toolchain version is retired and exact reproduction becomes impossible.
16. Evidence generation begins under subject revision A and finishes after revision B becomes current.
17. A cold archive is restored after years; age alone must not imply currentness.
18. A forged/misbound artifact has a valid storage object but wrong evidence-context binding.

## Proof obligations

1. Prove claim/provenance/raw-artifact retention can evolve independently without semantic relabeling.
2. Prove artifact identity survives URI/storage-tier changes.
3. Prove retained digest does not imply artifact availability or semantic correctness.
4. Prove freshness is evaluated against all proof-class-material horizons.
5. Prove raw pruning cannot silently preserve a qualification that requires raw reproducibility.
6. Prove old FAIL/PARTIAL/UNKNOWN dispositions survive supersession/compaction historically.
7. Prove a newer PASS does not rewrite historical evidence.
8. Prove privacy-driven purge changes reproducibility disposition explicitly.
9. Prove accessibility evidence binds evaluation scope/support baseline/profile, not scan output alone.
10. Prove storage outage is distinguishable from policy pruning and integrity failure.
11. Prove recovery evidence preserves unresolved effect/currentness/authority semantics.
12. Prove active-index compaction does not cap or omit historical causal lineage.
13. Prove evidence produced across a subject-revision race is bound to the actually evaluated revision or rejected/requalified.
14. Prove manual evidence states its reproducibility limitations.
15. Prove aggregate currentness never hides a mandatory stale/expired dimension.
16. Prove retention policy changes themselves are revisioned/auditable and cannot silently delete required evidence.

## Maturity / saturation

Disposition: `MATERIAL_DELTA / PARTIALLY_MATURE / NON_EXECUTABLE`.

The research now has a coherent chain from component fixtures -> multidimensional conformance -> evidence invalidation -> provenance/retention/freshness. Material gaps remain around evidence trust domains, third-party qualification/import, release/promotion gating over partial evidence, and long-term migration of evidence schemas/profile identities.

## Next research vector

Highest-value next vector:

**third-party/imported conformance evidence + trust-domain qualification and release/promotion gating**.

Questions:

- when can externally produced evidence contribute to an SB conformance claim;
- how producer identity, proof scope and profile compatibility are qualified;
- how imported evidence remains `UNTRUSTED/UNQUALIFIED/PARTIAL/CURRENT` without boolean trust collapse;
- how release/promotion gates consume multidimensional current evidence without treating one aggregate score as truth;
- how revoked/compromised producer trust invalidates downstream claims without rewriting historical facts.
